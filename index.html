Build, test, and challenge an argument

Construct a claim from premises, map objections and responses, and distinguish formal validity from the truth of premises. This tool visualizes reasoning; it does not automatically establish whether an argument is sound.

DEDUCTIVE LOGIC

Validity tester

For classical propositional logic, an argument is valid if there is no truth-value assignment making every premise true and its conclusion false. The checker supports P, Q, R, NOT (¬ or !), AND (∧ or &), OR (∨ or |), and IMPLIES (→ or ->), with parentheses. It does not assess whether real-world premises are true.
